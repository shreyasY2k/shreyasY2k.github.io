Praxis

The platform for your AI workforce.

Visit live product

Praxis builds agents from a versioned spec, proves they're ready (red-team tested, evaluated, policy-bound), and runs them inside existing business processes next to the human team, with one audit trail across every step.

Golang · TypeScript · AgentScope · LiteLLM · Temporal · OPA · Keycloak

i.Identity-aware gateway

Every LLM and tool call passes one chokepoint that verifies identity, enforces role-based authorization, runs inline prompt-injection and jailbreak guardrails, and signs an audit event. Agents are non-human identities minting short-lived tokens. No standing keys, and suspension revokes issuance instantly. Joiner, mover and leaver lifecycle, access reviews and SCIM 2.0.

ii.Attribution & audit

Token spend and guardrail events attributed to the exact human or agent. Provider-agnostic routing with budgets. Humans, agents and service calls land on one signed, tamper-evident, object-locked timeline you can query for who did what, under which identity, against which model and data.

iii.Proof before production

The WARDEN red-team gate runs adversarial and functional tests mapped to the OWASP LLM and Agentic Top 10, MITRE ATT&CK, GDPR and the EU AI Act, and can block a deploy. Agent Evals grade runs on versioned datasets. A Policy Engine compiles plain-English rules to Rego, enforced fail-closed by OPA.

iv.Build & run

Agents are declared as a spec plus a role file, generated onto AgentScope, and each runs as its own service. Risky tools pause for human approval. A Workflow Composer runs business processes as durable Temporal graphs, with a human gate on every state-changing call. Driven by a CLI and a builder skill for Claude Code and Cursor.

OathKeeper intelligent RPA for BFSI operations In progress

Today you can automate an SOP with RPA, but the intelligence is lost: the factors that made a BPS operator take a decision never make it into the bot. OathKeeper keeps that oath. It captures operators working across Java, C#, web, mail and mainframe applications, with redaction applied at capture and every step audited, and moves each process through four stages until the automation runs it end to end. The recorded behaviour, screenshots and sessions then train a foundation model to decide the next action itself, cutting human-in-the-loop down to the decisions that truly need a person.

Ontologic an AI co-designed knowledge graph

Domain experts and an AI co-designer build an ontology together. The AI proposes classes and relations, a named human confirms each one, and the approved model ships as validated Turtle onto ArcadeDB, with Apache Jena as the reasoning layer. Bi-temporal provenance, append-only corrections and a single validated write path mean the system can say what it believed at any past moment, what is missing, and where sources disagree.

About

I started at IHX as an early engineer, processing millions of medical documents a day on systems that weren't allowed to go down. I owned whatever needed owning, from the Kubernetes clusters to the observability stack to the login flow, and learned that the infrastructure around a product is usually where the hard problems live.

That carried straight into AI. The agent logic is often the easy part. Who the agent is, what it's allowed to touch, what it cost, and whether you can prove afterwards what it did: that's where the real engineering is. It's what I build at Mphasis, what I built Praxis to do, and what I keep writing about.
