A bit about me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer who specialises in agentic AI systems and cloud-native infrastructure. Started my career at IHX processing millions of medical documents daily, where I learned what it means to build systems that can't go down. Moved to Mphasis Applied AI to see how AI products get built and sold to enterprise — and ended up building ATOM, an agent platform I initially designed as an OSS project that's now running in production for BFSI clients.

I care most about the control plane around agents — auth, policy enforcement, observability, isolation. The agent logic is often the easy part. The infra that makes it safe and auditable is where the real engineering is.
