A bit about me

I'm a Senior Software Engineer who specialises in agentic AI systems and cloud-native infrastructure. Started my career at IHX processing millions of medical documents daily, where I learned what it means to build systems that can't go down. Now at Mphasis Applied AI, I build ATOM — an on-premises agentic platform — and AEGIS, the governance and information-security control plane around it. Together they run 500+ agents handling over a million requests a day inside Mphasis.

I care most about the control plane around agents — auth, policy enforcement, observability, isolation. The agent logic is often the easy part. The infrastructure that makes it safe and auditable is where the real engineering is.
